01 About

Engineering for scale, not for demos

I am a Senior Software Engineer working on backend and distributed systems. Since 2019 I have been building the platform behind e-commerce pricing and assortment analysis — the kind of system where correctness, throughput and search relevance all have to hold at once.

Most of my work sits between data engineering and platform architecture: designing ingestion pipelines that stay reliable under load, modelling data so it can actually be queried, and shaping Elasticsearch indices that answer real product questions in milliseconds. Increasingly that also means applying AI where it earns its place — entity matching, classification and enrichment across messy catalogue data.

I care about the parts that outlive a sprint: clear service boundaries, code the next engineer can read, review culture, and written standards instead of tribal knowledge. I hold a Master's degree in Software Engineering & Information Systems, and worked across QA automation, Android and full-stack delivery before specialising in backend.

02 What I do

Where I add the most value

Six problems I have solved repeatedly in production.

Data acquisition at scale

Distributed crawling and ingestion: work queues, back-pressure, retry semantics and rate control, so a pipeline touching millions of records degrades gracefully instead of falling over.

Search & relevance

Elasticsearch mappings, analysers, aggregations and query tuning — designing indices around the questions the product actually asks, then keeping them fast as the corpus grows.

Pricing & assortment analytics

Turning raw competitor and catalogue data into decisions: price positioning, assortment gaps, movement detection and the alerting that makes any of it actionable.

API & platform architecture

NestJS services with clear boundaries and contracts, sensible caching layers, background workers, and the observability needed to debug a distributed system at 2am.

Data enrichment with AI

Applying NLP and ML where deterministic rules run out: product matching across inconsistent listings, classification, attribute extraction and de-duplication over messy real-world catalogues.

Quality & engineering standards

BDD and integration test automation, containerised test environments, code review practice and written standard operating procedures — so quality survives team growth.
